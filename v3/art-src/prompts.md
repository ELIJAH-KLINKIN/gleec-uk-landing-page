# Art prompts

Every generated image or video starts from a brief here, so the set stays
consistent. References live in `reference/`; generated files go to `inbox/`
(not committed), and approved ones are encoded into `public/art/`.

## Fixed rules for every asset
- Brand purple #8C41FF, graphite, silver. Our own G logo only, copied from the reference.
- No text, numbers, names or card-scheme logos (no Visa or Mastercard), and nothing that looks like another brand's card.
- Transparent background unless the section needs a stage colour.
- Soft key light from the top left, gentle rim light, soft contact shadow. Apple product-photography feel.

## 01 · Company cards (Cards section, product tile 7)
References: `reference/gleec-card-purple.png`, `reference/gleec-card-graphite.png`
Model: GPT Image 2.5 (or ChatGPT image), high quality, 3:2, transparent background.

> Use the two attached card designs exactly as they are: same colours, the same G logo, chip and contactless mark. Do not add any text, numbers, names or card-scheme logos.
> Create a premium studio product render for a fintech website, in the style of Apple product photography:
> – the purple card in front, turned about 20° and tilted about 10°; the graphite card behind it, higher and to the right, turned a little more;
> – real card thickness, crisp rounded corners, a satin finish on the purple card and a fine brushed-metal finish on the graphite card;
> – soft key light from the top left, a gentle rim light along the edges, a soft contact shadow below;
> – transparent background: no floor, no backdrop, nothing else in the frame;
> – landscape 3:2, the cards filling about 70% of the frame.
